About Me

Hello, and welcome to my website! I am an amateur web developer; my skills today are perhaps somewhat limited, but as I learn and expand my abilities, this simple website will gain sophistication.

My background is humble: I am a first generation immigrant to the USA, from Mexico. My family arrived here when I was nine years old, so this would have been in the year 2005. I am grateful that I was able to study in the USA, though my performance in school was mostly unremarkable. Truthfully, I was somewhat pessimistic - my immigration status capped my dreams, the reality too grave to allow my ambitions to soar. I received a DACA work authorization permit, and without a clear direction, began working where I could. I eventually began to work for a food manufacturing company, and within their sales and supply-chain operations, there was a dire need for organization and process control.

Dreaming of the ways in which I would like data to flow, the results I wanted to derive from raw data imports, I began my learning journey. I began, naturally, first by mastering Excel. I learned it through and through, from nested to cube formulas; I learned to use Power Query and edit its M code to achieve complex transformations and formatting. I learned database principles, and learned to arrange my own relational databases within Excel. When I was able to combine this, then automate with VBA code, that was the closest I have been to feeling as if I had supernatural magic powers. It was very exciting, immediately gratifying. I improved through the years as I created new concept workbooks, new tools; I had a lot of room to experiment and practice.

Today, I am encouraging myself to dream further, way past the confines of the corporate building in which I worked. I am taking the principles that I have learned and expanding them out, so that I may find new ways to be creative, to make magic. At this point, I know the aforementioned data and programming skills, and now I am on journey to master web development skills, starting with HTML, and eventually I will be getting to work with Javascript and other cool languages and tools. Keep checking back in, and you may see this little website evolve with me.

Outside of programming and web development, I am interested in culinary arts (I plan to create a website/app for foodies eventually), I play guitar, and I love to read and write. My moments of leisure are typically spent reading, playing my guitar, playing a video game, or spending time with friends. Thanks for your interest in knowing a little about me. :)
