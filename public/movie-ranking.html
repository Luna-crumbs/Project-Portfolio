The Greatest Movies, According to Luna

My top three movies of all time

Kiki's Delivery Service

My favorite movie is Kiki's. I like this movie because it is beautifully animated, and the story of self discovery is one that I can relate to. The music by Yumi Arai is seriously good.

Pulp Fiction

Pulp Fiction features some of the best acting and dialogue in any movie. The winding tale is as hilarious as it is shocking.

Nirvana The Band The Movie The Show

This movie caught me off guard. I went to the movie theater on a whim back in January of 2026, and was met with a regular list of cheesy movie titles, but this one was so different. I figured that a production bold enough to have such a silly name must be good, and it really lived up to this notion. It is one of the best comedies that I have seen in my life.
